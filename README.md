### 📖 The Short Version

Most teams have data everywhere and insight nowhere — pipelines run, signals get buried, and nobody trusts the number on the dashboard. I build the systems that fix that: from real-time streaming pipelines and ETL that clean and structure raw data, to multi-agent AI systems that reason over it.

I'm an **MSc Advanced Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence)** student at the **University of Leeds** and a B.Tech (ICT) graduate of DA-IICT, with a published IEEE paper on federated learning, a **Microsoft Certified: Fabric Data Engineer Associate (DP-700)** certification, and hands-on work across streaming data platforms, RAG and agentic AI on Azure AI Foundry. Based in Leeds, UK, and open to Data Engineering, Data Analyst and Data Science / AI roles.
